# Code of conduct

inspo belongs to the people who use it and the people who improve it. To keep it a good place:

## What we expect

- Respect, even when we disagree.
- Criticism of the code, never of the person.
- Patience with newcomers: we all asked the obvious once.
- Credit for other people's work, including the designers whose sites show up as references.

## What isn't accepted

- Insults, mockery, harassment or personal attacks.
- Sexual, violent or discriminatory language or images.
- Publishing someone's private data.
- Spam, or promotion nobody asked for.

## If something happens

Report it with GitHub's **Report content** on the comment or issue, or privately from the
**Security** tab. The maintainer can edit or delete comments, lock conversations and block
anyone who doesn't respect these rules.

Based on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
